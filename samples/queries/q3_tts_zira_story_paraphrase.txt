This is the story of a little white dog with black spots who hated taking baths. One day, when he heard the water running in the tub, he grabbed the scrubbing brush, carried it out to the back yard and buried it. Then he ran away from home. He played all day near the roads, at the railway, and around a building site, and he got so dirty that he turned into a black dog with white spots. When he finally came home hungry and tired, his family did not recognise him at all. So the dog dug up the brush he had hidden, ran upstairs and jumped into the bathtub himself. After a good scrub his spots appeared again, the family shouted that it was their dog, and he fell asleep happily on his bed, with the brush hidden under his pillow.
